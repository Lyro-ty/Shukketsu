# Assassination Mutilate Rogue PvE Guide

## Overview

The Mutilate spec is the primary Assassination build for PvE in TBC. Unlike Combat Swords,
Mutilate requires daggers in both main hand and off hand. The spec revolves around generating
combo points quickly through critical strikes and consuming Deadly Poison stacks with Envenom
for armor-ignoring Nature damage.

## Core Abilities

### Mutilate

Mutilate has an energy cost of 60 energy, making it more expensive per use than Sinister
Strike. It generates 2 base combo points per cast. Each Mutilate strikes twice: once with the
main hand and once with the off hand. With Seal Fate, each crit on either strike generates an
extra combo point. Since Mutilate hits twice (MH + OH), a double crit with Seal Fate can
generate up to 4 combo points from a single Mutilate cast. This makes it an extremely
efficient combo point generator when your crit rate is high.

### Envenom

Envenom is the key finishing move for Assassination rogues. It consumes Deadly Poison stacks
from the target (1 stack per combo point used) and deals Nature damage that scales with attack
power. Critically, Envenom damage ignores armor entirely, making it particularly effective
against heavily armored raid bosses where physical finishing moves suffer from mitigation.

## Poison Setup

The standard poison setup is Instant Poison on the main hand and Deadly Poison on the off
hand. In groups with a Shaman providing Windfury Totem, some rogues run Deadly Poison on the
off hand only, leaving the main hand free for Windfury procs. Deadly Poison mechanics: it has
a 30% proc rate, stacks up to 5 times on the target, and deals 180 Nature damage over 12
seconds per stack.

## Key Talents

The Assassination tree provides several critical talents for the Mutilate build:

- **Find Weakness** - after using a finishing move, exposes a weakness in the target that
  increases damage dealt for a short duration
- **Seal Fate** - grants an extra combo point whenever a combo point-generating ability
  critically strikes (the engine behind Mutilate's CP generation)
- **Cold Blood** - 3 minute cooldown ability that guarantees the next offensive ability crits;
  often paired with a 5-point Envenom for massive burst
- **Vile Poisons** - increases all poison damage by 20%, a huge multiplier for an
  Envenom-centric build

## Competitive Timing

Mutilate becomes competitive in Phase 3 and beyond with T6 gear, as the higher crit rates and
better daggers available in later tiers play directly into the spec's strengths. However, it
consistently trails Combat Swords in raw DPS output, though the gap narrows significantly with
better gear. Combat is superior in Phase 1 due to weapon availability and Combat Potency
energy economy, which Assassination lacks entirely.

## Stat Priority

The stat priority for Mutilate differs from Combat in important ways. Hit rating is less
valuable beyond the special cap because Assassination lacks Combat Potency; there is no
benefit from additional white hit since there is no Combat Potency to benefit from white hit
procs. Additionally, there is no fast off-hand requirement since Combat Potency is not a
factor. The spec instead favors raw agility and crit to maximize Seal Fate and Find Weakness
uptime.
