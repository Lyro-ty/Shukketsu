# Subtlety Rogue PvE Guide

## Overview

Subtlety is the weakest PvE spec for rogues in TBC but has raid utility that can justify a
spot in certain compositions. The spec trades personal DPS for energy efficiency and a
physical damage debuff that benefits the entire raid.

## Core Abilities

### Hemorrhage

Hemorrhage costs 35 energy, deals 110% weapon damage, and applies a debuff to the target
that adds +42 physical damage to each hit the target receives (lasting for 10 charges).
Comparing Hemorrhage vs Sinister Strike: Hemo costs 35 energy and deals 110% weapon damage,
while Sinister Strike costs 45 energy and deals weapon damage + 98 flat damage. Hemorrhage
has better energy efficiency (damage per energy point) but lower personal DPS overall.

### Shadowstep

Shadowstep is a 25 yard teleport to the target that also applies a +20% damage buff for the
next attack. This mobility tool is useful for encounters with movement phases.

### Preparation

Preparation resets all cooldowns, effectively doubling the availability of key abilities
during long encounters.

## Why Lower DPS Than Combat

Subtlety produces less personal damage than Combat for several reasons. The spec lacks both
Adrenaline Rush and Blade Flurry cooldowns, which are the primary burst and sustained DPS
drivers for Combat rogues. Additionally, Hemorrhage deals less damage per hit than Sinister
Strike in absolute terms despite its lower energy cost.

## Key Talents

The Subtlety tree provides several important talents:

- **Serrated Blades** - increases Rupture damage and allows it to ignore a portion of armor
- **Sinister Calling** - increases Agility by a percentage, boosting crit and attack power
- **Deadliness** - increases Attack Power by a percentage from all sources

## Rotation

The rotation priority for Subtlety follows a similar logic to Combat:

1. **Slice and Dice** - maintain maximum uptime
2. **Rupture** - keep ticking at 5 combo points
3. **Hemorrhage** - primary combo point generator
4. **Eviscerate** - use when Rupture and Slice and Dice are both active with long durations

## When Viable

Subtlety is viable in T4 and T5 content where DPS checks are lenient and the raid can afford
the personal DPS loss. The Hemorrhage debuff provides raid utility by adding +42 physical
damage per hit across all melee and ranged physical attackers, which in a melee-heavy raid
composition can partially offset the rogue's personal DPS loss.
