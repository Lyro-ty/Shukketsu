# Combat Swords Rogue PvE Guide

## Overview

Combat Swords is the dominant PvE spec for rogues in TBC. It offers the highest sustained
damage output across all raid tiers thanks to powerful cooldowns, efficient energy economy,
and strong scaling with gear. Every serious rogue should understand this spec thoroughly.

## Stat Priority

The stat priority for Combat Swords is:

**Expertise (to 26 cap) > Hit (to special cap) > Agility > Haste > Attack Power > Crit**

The expertise soft cap is 26 expertise skill (approximately 103 expertise rating). Reaching
this cap removes the 6.5% boss dodge chance entirely. The Weapon Expertise talent in the
Combat tree gives 10 free expertise skill, so rogues only need 16 from gear and buffs.

### Hit Rating

The hit cap is 9% total, which requires 142 hit rating. However, with the 5/5 Precision
talent providing 5% hit, only 4% from gear is needed (64 hit rating) to reach the special
cap. Yellow attacks (special abilities like Sinister Strike) have a 9% miss cap.

The dual-wield miss penalty only affects white (auto-attack) hits, not special (yellow)
attacks. White attacks suffer from a 28% DW miss penalty. Hit beyond the special cap still
reduces white miss rate, which is valuable for Combat rogues because of Combat Potency procs.

## Rotation

The rotation priority is straightforward:

1. **Slice and Dice** - maintain 100% uptime (this is the #1 priority at all times)
2. **Rupture** at 5 combo points - keep this ticking on the boss
3. **Sinister Strike** - filler ability to generate combo points

Sinister Strike costs 45 energy base. With 2/2 Improved Sinister Strike, the cost drops to
40 energy, though most optimal builds skip this talent in favor of other points.

## Cooldown Stacking

For burst phases, stack cooldowns together: **Blade Flurry + Adrenaline Rush + Haste Potion**.
This combination produces massive sustained burst damage during the window.

- **Blade Flurry**: 2 minute cooldown, 15 second duration, 25 energy cost. Hits a second
  nearby target for 100% damage.
- **Slice and Dice**: increases attack speed by 30%.

## Energy Economy

Base energy regeneration is 20 energy per 2 seconds (10 energy per second). Combat rogues
supplement this with **Combat Potency**: a 20% chance on each successful off-hand melee hit
to restore 15 energy. This is why fast off-hand weapons are preferred (1.4s speed is better
than 1.8s) for more Combat Potency procs. Latro's Shifting Sword is the preferred off-hand
weapon due to its fast 1.4-second swing speed.

## Phase 1 Best in Slot Trinkets

The Phase 1 BiS trinkets are:

- **Dragonspine Trophy** from Gruul's Lair: procs +325 haste rating (~20.6% haste) at
  approximately 1 PPM. This trinket remains competitive deep into TBC across multiple tiers.
- **Bloodlust Brooch** from badges: a strong on-use trinket that pairs well with cooldowns.

## Enchants and Consumables

**Mongoose enchant** provides +120 agility and +30 haste rating for 15 seconds at roughly
1 PPM. When applied to both weapons, the procs can dual stack for enormous burst potential.

Key consumables include:

- **Haste Potion** - used during Blade Flurry + Adrenaline Rush windows
- **Flask of Relentless Assault** - provides +120 attack power
- **Warp Burger** - provides +20 agility
- **Adamantite Weightstone** - provides +12 weapon damage

## Raid Buffs

Key raid buffs that significantly boost rogue DPS:

- **Windfury Totem** - 20% chance on each melee hit for an extra attack
- **Blessing of Might** - provides +220 attack power
- **Leader of the Pack** - provides +5% critical strike chance

## Expose Armor vs Sunder Armor

Expose Armor with 2/2 Improved Expose Armor reduces target armor by 3075, compared to
Sunder Armor at 5 stacks which only reduces armor by 2600. These are mutually exclusive
debuffs (they cannot both be active), so in raids with warrior tanks the group must
coordinate which debuff to use.
